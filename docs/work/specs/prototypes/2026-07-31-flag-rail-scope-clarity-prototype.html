100-group case

At scale, the grid itself is unaffected (marks are per-cell, drawn identically regardless of grid size — not shown in full here, only illustrated as a strip). The rail is where scale actually shows up: dozens of "This week overall" rows. Scope-grouping keeps the short, actionable "On this grid" list from being buried in a long aggregate tail.
