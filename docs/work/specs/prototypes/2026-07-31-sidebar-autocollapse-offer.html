It asks on the edge, not on load

The offer fires once, on the transition from short to complete. It does not reappear on every launch, and it never fires while setup is still short.

Either answer is final

Tuck away folds the section and remembers folded. Keep open leaves it and remembers that too — the offer does not come back next week to ask again. Both write setupFoldOffered: true; only the fold state differs.

The header still toggles

Whatever they answered, clicking the header works as it always did. A remembered answer sets the starting state; it never takes the control away.

If setup breaks again, nothing jumps

Delete the days on a folded sidebar and the header goes to ! 5 / 6 in place. It does not re-expand — layout moving under a director is the thing we were avoiding, and the roll-up already carries the alert.

Per device, not synced

Both values live in localStorage. One director folding their sidebar shouldn't fold a counsellor's on another laptop.
