Collapsing must not hide a problem

The conflicts badge rolls up onto the System header, and ! 4 / 6 stays on Camp Set Up. A director who tidied their sidebar in June still sees a sync problem in August. This is the rule that makes collapsing safe — without it, collapse is a way to lose alerts.

Chevron is always visible

Twenty hides its chevron until you hover the section. Cleaner, and wrong for this audience — a director who doesn't know sections collapse will never hover to find out. Low-contrast and always there.

The whole header is the target

Not just the ▶. A 216px-wide row is an easy click; a 12px glyph is not. aria-expanded tracks state, and the header is a real button so it takes keyboard focus and Enter/Space.

Open/closed is remembered per device

localStorage, never synced — how one director likes their sidebar is not camp data, and shouldn't change what a counsellor sees on another laptop.

Not tabs

Tabs would show one section at a time. Collapse lets a director keep Schedule open while Camp Set Up stays folded — the common August case — and nothing forces a choice between them.
